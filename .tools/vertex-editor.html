Ship Vertex Editor

Instructions:
  1. Place your ship image in this folder and update IMAGE_PATH in filedef.js
  2. Click on the image to add vertices (points) around the ship outline
  3. Click vertices in order (clockwise or counter-clockwise)
  4. The polygon will close automatically when you click "Finish"
  5. Copy the console output and give it to me
Tips: The center of the image is (0, 0). Click "Undo" to remove the last point. Click "Clear" to start over.
Mirror Mode: Draw the left half of your ship, then click "Mirror" to auto-complete the right side.
